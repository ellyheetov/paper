영문 기사를 읽고 영어로 요약하는 연습용 틀이다. 기사 내용 정리와 영어 공부를 한 번에 하는 게 목표.

## 📰 Article

- **Headline**: Write the headline here
- **Source / Date**: Outlet, publish date
- **Link**: Article URL

## ✍️ Summary in English

Summarize the article in three sentences, in your own words. Try not to copy phrases directly from the article.

1. What happened?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What might happen next?

## 🔤 Key Expressions

| Expression | 뜻 | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *rein in* | 억제하다, 통제하다 | The central bank moved to rein in inflation. |
| *on the heels of* | ~의 직후에 | The decision came on the heels of weak jobs data. |
| *weigh on* | ~에 부담을 주다 | Higher rates continue to weigh on housing demand. |

## 💬 My Take

Write one or two sentences about what you think, in English.
